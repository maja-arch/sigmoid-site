Design system

Button proposals

Three shapes taken from the logo: the faceted, polygonal symbol and the decagon icons. All three use the same colours and type: Roboto 500, uppercase, 13px. Hover over them to see the states.

Option A

Facet

All four corners are cut at 45°, like the facets on the decagon icons. It reads as angular but stays calm and readable, so it is the safest choice for the whole site.

Option B

Prism

Pointed ends, like an elongated hexagon. This is the most angular option and the most recognisable, but it is louder and takes more horizontal space. Best kept for one or two main CTAs.
