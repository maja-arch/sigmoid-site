{ Flexible first steps }

Scope, team and collaboration model stay custom. We agree on a useful first step and learn from the work together.

  1. 01

    Trial / initial engagement

    A shorter initial period to assess ways of working, communication and technical fit.

  2. 02

    Limited specialist scope

    A defined part of the project that addresses a specific need and can open space for broader collaboration.

  3. 03

    Prototype / PoC

    Validation of a concept or technical solution before a larger investment in development.

  4. 04

    Pilot scope

    A limited initial phase before a longer-term or broader engagement.
